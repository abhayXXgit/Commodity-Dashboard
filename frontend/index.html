TransformerProcure Intelligence — commodity price tracking, forecasting and transformer BOM cost exposure for power and solar transformer procurement.
Recommendations are procurement decision-support signals derived from price, forecast, volatility, BOM exposure and coverage data. They are not financial or investment advice. Forecasts are statistical projections with back-tested error bands, not guarantees. Every price carries its source, timestamp, currency, unit and basis; data classes (live, verified historical, supplier quote, estimated, forecast, demo) are never blurred.
